Now playing and album art

Now playing and album art are part of PanelOS Pro. Lite shows the stats only.

It is not only Spotify. PanelOS can follow any player that appears in the Windows media controls, including YouTube Music in Chrome or Edge, Apple Music, Tidal, and the Spotify desktop app. There is no Spotify login in PanelOS.

In Settings, under Now playing, pick the source:

  • Auto follows the current Windows media session, then a Spotify session, then any other session.
  • SMTC stays on the Windows media session.
  • Spotify only accepts a Spotify session. Keep the Spotify desktop app open on the same PC.

The first-run setup wizard also has a Music step with the same three choices, from version 1.4.4.

Album art is looked up by song title and artist through the public iTunes and Deezer search APIs. While that search runs, and if no match is found, PanelOS uses a cleaned thumbnail from the player. A thumbnail that is mostly a logo or a flat color is dropped.
