Themes

Lite: Classic only, no now-playing. Pro: every theme, with now-playing where the layout uses it.

  • Signature: full-bleed cover, dark left plate, large CPU/GPU numbers (default on Pro).
  • Nature Flow: stats over a quiet nature wallpaper slide.
  • Noir Editorial: one number large; the rest stays quiet.
  • Gallery: cover fills the screen; stats as a bottom caption.
  • Chronograph: two dials for CPU and GPU.
  • Classic: dense stats + cover; the Lite theme.

Switch from the tray → Theme. The panel updates on the next frame. Landscape is 480×320; portrait is 320×480; pick either in Settings.
