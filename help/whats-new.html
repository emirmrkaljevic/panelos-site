What's new

Updates are free for all 1.x versions. Use Update in the tray menu, or download the latest installer and run it over the old one. Settings and your Pro license are kept.

Download the latest installer · All releases and SHA-256 checksums

1.4.3 (November 10, 2026)

  • Fan selection now stays selected in Settings after Save and apply.
  • Sparklines in Signature, Noir, Nature Flow and the theme editor show real CPU and GPU load.

1.4.2 (November 10, 2026)

  • Fixes the FPS reading in games: restores the frame counting from 1.3.5 and fixes game mode staying on with an empty FPS tile.

1.4.1 (November 10, 2026)

  • Fixes the missing FPS reading in game mode: FPS now follows the process that actually draws the game (launchers, DirectX 12 and Vulkan games).

1.4.0 (November 9, 2026)

  • 5 inch 800×480 screen support (beta), a theme editor for your own layouts (Pro), and game mode that switches to a gaming theme with FPS while you play.

1.3.5 (November 7, 2026)

  • Live FPS on all themes (Show FPS in Settings). Update menu fixes.

1.3.4 (October 31, 2026)

  • The red temperature alert clears once CPU and GPU are 2°C under the limit. Raising the limit or turning the alert off applies at once.
  • A quick screen update no longer leaves stale pixels from an update that was not sent yet.

1.3.3 (October 31, 2026)

  • The forecast on the weather background is about twice as big: bold white temperatures on a rounded panel, placed so it does not cover the song or the stats. Tight layouts use one line.
  • Small grey readings, such as load and watts, are brighter on the weather background.

1.3.2 (October 31, 2026)

  • With Background set to Weather, the weather stays behind every theme while music plays. The album cover shows only as the small song thumbnail, where a theme has one.
  • New weather skies for sun, clouds, rain, snow, fog and storms, by day and by night, including a deep blue starry night. Text gets a soft shadow so it stays readable.

1.3.1 (October 31, 2026)

  • Save & apply stays visible at the bottom of Settings.
  • Choosing a city or changing the background saves, fetches weather and updates the screen at once.
  • City suggestions appear from 2 letters. Pick one with the mouse, or with the arrow keys and Enter.
  • New tray item: Check for updates. Automatic checks run every hour.

1.3.0 (October 31, 2026)

  • The weather line stays visible. A long line shortens to the city and temperature, for example "Berlin 8°C", and it shows while music plays. Weather is fetched right after Save.
  • The city field suggests matching places as you type, shown as city and country, for example "Berlin, Germany". Picking one stores that place so the lookup is exact.
  • New Background option next to the city: album cover or weather.
  • Pro: an animated weather scene behind the clock (sun, clouds, rain, snow, fog, storms), sky colours that follow the time of day, a 3 hour and tomorrow forecast, an accent colour from the album cover, and a red edge glow when CPU or GPU reaches a temperature you set.
  • Settings and the license key are kept across updates.

1.2.0 (October 29, 2026)

  • Themes can show a local clock (time and date) and weather for the city you type in Settings. Weather comes from Open-Meteo, refreshes every 15 minutes, and keeps the last reading when the network is down.
  • Now playing can follow any app through Windows media controls. Auto uses that session, then Spotify. Settings can use Auto, the Windows session, or Spotify only.

1.1.0 (October 26, 2026)

  • Lite now shows a preview of the Pro themes in Settings, with a Get Pro button.
  • If no screen is found at startup, PanelOS shows one notice with a link to help.
  • New Release notes item in the tray menu.

1.0.2 (October 20, 2026)

  • The tray Update item now turns on as soon as an update is found.
  • Your Pro license is kept across updates and reinstalls.

1.0.1 (October 19, 2026)

  • New tray Update item: PanelOS checks for new versions and installs them in one click.

1.0.0 (October 19, 2026)

  • First public release. Lite (free): Classic theme. Pro: all six themes, Spotify now-playing, 2 PCs, all 1.x updates.
  • CPU, GPU, RAM and VRAM readings match Task Manager.
  • The screen turns off when the PC shuts down, restarts or sleeps, or when PanelOS exits, and comes back on when the PC wakes. No BIOS changes needed.
